HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

  1. You are a mail pilot. Fly along the valley or the coast.
  2. When a village or island is close, a yellow tip appears at the bottom. Press E to drop a parcel.
  3. Aim so the parcel lands near the glowing ring above the village. Closer is a perfect drop.
  4. Deliver 5 parcels to finish the Courier Run. No timer, take your time.

NICE TO KNOW

  • Fly through golden wind rings to build your combo and fill the boost bar.
  • Skim low over water, or fly under a stone bridge, for extra boost.
  • Crashing into a building breaks your plane. You respawn a moment later and keep your parcels. Turn on Crash protection in Settings if you would rather not crash.

CONTROLS

On a phone or tablet: steer with the thumb stick on the left, and use the DROP and BOOST buttons on the right. Turn the phone sideways.
